🐈 Sprite Lab · 貓咪動作對照工作台

A workbench for filling in cat-frames.js. Your PNG is read by your own browser and never leaves your computer.
這是給維護者用的工具頁，訪客看不到。圖片只在你自己的瀏覽器裡開啟，不會上傳。

1Load a cat sheet · 載入貓咪圖檔

Pick any of the four cat PNGs (they all share the identical layout, so inventorying ONE of them covers all four colours).
四隻貓的排版完全相同，所以只需要對照其中一張。

2One frame at a time · 單張畫格

What you are looking at: the square on the left is a single <div>. It is 32×32 pixels of the big picture, blown up. Changing Row/Col just slides the big picture behind that window — the image file is never cut. This is exactly how the cat will be drawn on the real site.

3Find your animations · 找出各個動作

Every row of the sheet is listed below. Click a row to load it above, then press Play and watch what the cat does. The “frames” number is counted automatically by looking for blank squares — it is usually right, but trust your eyes over it.
點一列 → 上面按播放 → 看貓在做什麼。「frames」是自動數出來的。

Load a sheet first ↑

🚫 Rows shaded orange are the hiss and sad animations. This project deliberately does not use them — the pet never guilt-trips anyone. Please leave them alone.

4Copy the line into cat-frames.js · 複製貼上

Load a sheet and pick a row.

Open cat-frames.js, find the line with the same nickname, and replace it with this one. The four nicknames already in that file (eat-front, sleep-lay-front-l, itch-r, wash-sit) must keep their exact spelling — other files ask for them by name.

Done? Save cat-frames.js, then reload this page and spot-check a couple of rows to be sure. Nothing here changes the live site — the live site only reads cat-frames.js.
